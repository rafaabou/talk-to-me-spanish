# Privacy Policy — Talk To Me Spanish

**Last updated**: September 7, 2026
**App**: Talk To Me Spanish
**Developer**: Mohamed Rafaa Tounekti

---

## Overview

Talk To Me Spanish is a **100% offline** language learning app. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

---

## Data Collection

**We collect nothing.** The app does not:

- Collect personal information
- Track your usage
- Send analytics
- Use cookies
- Access your contacts
- Access your location
- Access your camera or microphone (audio is pre-bundled, not recorded)
- Make any network connections

---

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device only**:

| Data Type | Storage Location | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| Lesson progress | SharedPreferences | Track completed lessons |
| Exercise results | SharedPreferences | Track completed exercises |
| Flashcard state | SharedPreferences | FSRS spaced repetition scheduling |
| Favorites | SharedPreferences | Bookmarked vocabulary |
| Day streak | SharedPreferences | Daily study tracking |
| Quiz scores | SharedPreferences | Best scores per level |
| Lesson notes | SharedPreferences | Your personal notes |
| App settings | SharedPreferences | Theme, language, audio speed |
| Progress export | User-initiated file | Backup/restore |

**None of this data ever leaves your device.**

| Permission | Status | Reason |

## Audio Content

All 5,932 audio pronunciation files are generated during development and bundled into the APK. They are not downloaded at runtime. No audio is recorded from the user.

---

## Web Version

The web version runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server by the app. Progress is stored in browser local storage.

**One browser exception:** if you use the microphone in Chrome or Edge, the *browser's* built-in speech recognition (the Web Speech API) may process the audio on the browser vendor's servers — that is a browser feature the app does not control, and it is why the app also offers record-and-self-check without speech recognition. The app itself never uploads audio, and the Android app has no `INTERNET` permission at all, so on-device speech recognition stays on the phone. Firefox has no speech recognition support; the app detects this and switches to record, replay and self-check.
